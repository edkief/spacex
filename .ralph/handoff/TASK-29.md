## Dead ends

- Starting the approach with VTOL held the whole way: the ×0.5/tick assist (100 m range) kills all momentum within ~1 s / ~3 m, so the ship hovers 97 m out and never reaches the pad. The assist must only be held in the final phase.
- Thrusting horizontally in the atmosphere to close distance: does not exist in the flight model (thrust is space-only) — do not "fix" this; it's the TASK-22 design and the spec's approach script works via initial momentum + drag + final VTOL braking.
- VTOL-only takeoff: VTOL_LIFT === GRAVITY, so a docked ship holding VTOL stays neutrally buoyant on the pad forever (vel.y stays 0). The takeoff test must inject vertical velocity as state.
- Using the raw chunk (0,0) pad coordinates (near the world origin) as pad positions: ~10 km outside the planet's atmosphere anchor → surface regime unreachable → docking impossible. Hence anchor + offset.
