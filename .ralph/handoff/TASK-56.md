## Dead ends
- **Vitest hang:** the first run of `guidance.test.ts` hung past 60 s with no output. Cause: `vi.useFakeTimers({ toFake: [] })` in the store describe's `beforeEach` without a matching `useRealTimers` — vitest 4 appears to stall on worker teardown. Fixed by removing fake timers entirely (the store uses none). Don't reintroduce fake timers without `useRealTimers` in `afterEach`.
- **Claims screen "rapid typing" test failure:** asserted the probe fired with `callsign=final-one` but had only typed up to `fin` — a test bug (wrong expected string), fixed in the test, not the code. The debounce behavior itself (1 probe for N rapid keystrokes) was correct.
- **Guidance spawn-dock trap:** a naive docked→step-2 transition would fire immediately at boot (the starter ship IS docked at the home dock). Solved with the `airborne` flag in `guidanceAdvance` (see `guidance.ts` header comment). If the e2e shows the hint stuck on step 1 after a real dock, check that `undocked` events actually fire — they come from the docked-transition detection in `useGameSession`'s entity_update handler (main.tsx, around the `setDockedIndicator` call).
- **e2e `?sys=` param:** still honored (join target override), unchanged.
