## Dead ends

- **Firing while docked and expecting the flash to be visible WITHOUT the FX fix: impossible.** The beam is a 1-px `THREE.Line` from the docked scout's nose; its direction is deterministic per seed and points largely along the camera's line-of-sight (the far tip projects onto the planet/star at screen center), and `depthTest` occluded it; the 1.2-unit muzzle spark is sub-pixel at the ~164-unit camera distance. The spec's "re-shoot if no flash in the png" remedy does NOT work here (every shot is the same direction) — the FX fix above is the real solution.
- **Color-scan for laser red (255,80,64) in the central 25–75% region: 0 pixels pre-fix**, and fragile post-fix (additive blending over varying background; the HUD uses similar reds `#ff9a76`/`#e05c42` bottom-right — keep scans away from the corners).
- **Baseline-vs-flash full-canvas pixel diff: ~9,500 changed pixels, but dominated by the slowly rotating starfield** (`background.stars.rotation.y`, 0.005 rad/s) — not a clean signal without extra filtering (raise threshold or filter to warm pixels only).
- **Debug technique that DID work** (probes removed; re-add if needed): TEMP dev-only `get __debug__()` returning `{ scene, camera }` on `WorldManager` + `window.__WORLD__ = worldRef.current` in `main.tsx` right after manager creation; from the e2e, walk `scene.children` and project `o.position.clone().project(camera)` (fx objects have identity parent, so local == world; filter `o.material.blending === 2` for AdditiveBlending). That revealed the spark's screen position/scale/opacity definitively.
