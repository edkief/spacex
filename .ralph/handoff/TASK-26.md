## Dead ends
- `generateSurfaceChunk` alone costs ≈8.5 ms on this machine (bench: 20-chunk loop) — one call can never fit the 4 ms slice → the staged row-batched `ChunkBuild` (8 rows/unit ≈1 ms) via the new `heightfieldChannels` export.
- `THREE.BufferGeometry.computeVertexNormals()` for near+mid costs ≈7 ms — too slow → analytic normals from central differences over the height grid (cheaper and deterministic).
- The far ring (2048–8000 m) CANNOT be fully covered: the full 8 km annulus is ≈2600 chunks vs the 400-chunk LRU cap. Far impostors are therefore a low-priority window (Chebyshev radius 6 ≈ 2.7 km horizon) dropped first under backlog. Document this tension in the final log; a true 8 km horizon needs the TASK-58 tuning pass (bigger cap / bigger impostor cells).
- Spec says "512 m cells" but TASK-5's terrain chunks are 320 m (64 cells × 5 m) and the golden fixtures pin that — kept 320 m and documented in the chunks.ts header: the 3×3 near block's far corners sit at 452 m, inside the 512 m near distance, so the near ring matches the spec.
- "5x5 neighborhood (13 chunks)" is self-contradictory (5×5=25) — implemented as 3×3 + 4 cardinals at distance 2 (13 chunks within a 5×5 span); 7×7=49 when fast (≥100 m/s, per the pop-in criterion).
- Index buffers: Uint32 vs Uint16 — near grid is 4225 verts so Uint16 is safe and halves the index memory (72 MB total vs ~99 MB at 3×2 bytes… see RING_BYTES).
