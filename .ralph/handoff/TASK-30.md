## Dead ends
- Using `pad.pos` (shared sim coords, anchor at x=10000) for streamer/character positions → `invalid chunk key: NaN,-3` crash. Must use `padLocal = pad.pos − anchor` (local chunk metres).
- Bare `!import.meta.env.DEV` guard in the hook → TypeError under tsx (Node has no `import.meta.env`). Use `import.meta.env?.DEV` + `typeof window` guard.
- Budgeting the delta against the phase's generation-IDLE steady frames makes the chunk-spike phase structurally red (the 4 ms slice cap IS the spike). The steady-streaming-control baseline (busy-frame p50) is the designed remedy — do not instead relax the budget or use raw max-of-raw (loses regression signal).
- Do NOT add `streamerTravelM`-style unsigned distance: descent moves −X toward the pad, climb +X. Signed `streamerDeltaM` per segment, with an exact landing snap on the last frame.
